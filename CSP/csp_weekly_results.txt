-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Interpretation
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A  Sells the full daily harvest (~160 MWh_e/day), holding only through the
   deep midday dip; liquidates the initial 70 MWh_th on Monday and refills to
   the floor on Sunday.  Zero grid purchases: the tank is already full of FREE
   solar exactly during the cheap midday hours, so midday arbitrage has no
   room.  Lowest continuation value (sunny future, 60 EUR/MWh).

B  Sunny days behave like A.  On the two overcast days (Wed, Sun) the diffuse
   harvest is small, the tank has spare room and there is no PV-glut dip, so
   the plant buys 297.1 MWh_e of cheap midday power and resells it from TES
   into the evening peak.  Continuation value roughly doubles vs A.

C  The diffuse-only harvest is tiny (441.5 MWh_th/week).  With no national PV
   the midday price stays moderate under a high evening peak (spread above
   the ~3.3x round-trip threshold), so the plant acts as a battery: ~136
   MWh_e/day of cheap midday purchases, resold into the evening peak — about
   64 % of its exports are grid-origin.  Sells only into the peaks (avg
   105.5 EUR/MWh_e).  Highest continuation value (dark future, 90 EUR/MWh).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Verification notes
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Energy balance closes in every scenario:
    exports / 0.38 = solar_heat + 0.8 * purchases - leak (+/- tank delta)
  e.g. C: 455.6 / 0.38 = 1198.9 = 441.5 + 0.8 * 952.9 - 4.8.
* The implied cut slopes reproduce the model formula
    a1 = eta_power * future_sell_price * max(0, 1 - mean(future_solar_forecast)):
    A 0.38 * 60 * (1 - 0.365) = 14.48
    B 0.38 * 70 * (1 - 0.242) = 20.17
    C 0.38 * 90 * (1 - 0.055) = 32.33
* theta_future = a1 * q_end exactly in all scenarios (tank ends at the 70
  MWh_th floor).
* "min -0.0" in the TES line is the IEEE-754 negative-zero display of an exact
  0.0 (the tank legitimately empties during the week); cosmetic only.
